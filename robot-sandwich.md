# How to Make a Peanut Butter Sandwich

## Assimptions

- The robot is standing in front of the kitchen counter.
- The robot's hands sre empty.
- A bag of sliced bread is on the counter.
- A closed jar of peanut butter is on the counter.
- A clean butter knife is on the counter.
- A clean plate is on the hands.
- The robot can use both hands.
- The sandwich will use two slices of bread.

## Steps

1. Pick up the bag of bread.
2. Open the bands are empty.
3. Remove one slice of bread from the bag.
4. Place the slice of bread flat on the plate.
5. Remove a second slice of bread from the bag.
6. Place the second slice of bread flat on the plate next to the first slice.
7. Put the bread bag back on the counter.
8. Pick up the peanut butter jar.
9. Hold the peanut butter jar with one hand.
10. Grip the lid of the peanut butter jar with the other hand.
11. Turn the lid counterclockwise until the lid separates from the jar.
12. Place the lid on the counter.
13. Place the open peanut butter jar on the counter.
14. Pick up the butter knife by the handle.
15. Hold the knife with the blade pointing away from your hand.
16. Move the knife blade into the open peanut butter jar.
17. Scoop peanut butter onto the flat side of the knife blade.
18. Remove the knife from the jar.
19. Move the knife above the first slice of bread.
20. Touch the peanut better on the knife to the top surface of the first slice of bread.
21. Move the knife across the top surface of the bread to spread the peanut butter.
22. Continue spreading until the top surface of the first slice has as even layer of peanut butter.
23. Lift the knife away from the bread.
24. Place the knife on the counter.
25. Pick up the second slice of bread.
26. Move the second slice directly above the first slice.
27. Lower the second alice onto the peanut butter-covered side of the first slice.
28. Align the edges of the two bread slices.
29. Release the second slice.
30. Pick up the peanut butter jar lid.
31. Place the lid on top of the peanut butter jar.
32. Turn the lid clockwise until it is closed.
33. Put the closed peanut butter jar on the counter.

## Done When

the task is complete when two slices of bread are stacked together with peanut butter between them, the sandwich is the plate, and the peanut butter jar is closed.
